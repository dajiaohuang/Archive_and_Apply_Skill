# Academic Application Workflow

Use this reference for graduate, professional-school, fellowship, and scholarship applications.

## Program brief first

Before drafting, capture:

- institution, department, degree, track, and intake term
- official application URL
- deadline and time zone
- exact prompts and upload fields
- word, character, page, and file-format limits
- required and optional documents
- recommender count and eligibility rules
- faculty-contact expectations, if any
- retrieval date for time-sensitive requirements

Program and department instructions override all bundled templates.

## Prompt-to-evidence map

For each prompt component, record:

- what the prompt asks
- candidate evidence and source path
- intended takeaway
- missing fact or research task
- target word budget only after the official limit is known

Draft from this map, not directly from a generic template.

## Document boundaries

Names vary by program. Determine purpose from the prompt.

- **Academic/statement of purpose:** preparation, intellectual direction, goals, and program fit.
- **Personal history/personal statement:** formative context, perspective, values, barriers, service, or community contribution when the prompt requests them.
- **Research statement/proposal:** research questions, prior work, methods, results, and future agenda.
- **Writing sample/portfolio:** a selected artifact governed by the program's instructions.

When multiple statements are required, allocate evidence so each answers its own prompt. Some overlap in chronology is acceptable; duplicated paragraphs are not.

## Fit research

Verify faculty, lab, curriculum, facilities, and program structure from current official sources. Explain a concrete intellectual connection. Do not merely swap names across applications, and do not imply that a faculty member is accepting students unless an authoritative current source says so.

## Publications and research status

Preserve exact status: published, accepted, in press, under review, submitted, preprint, or work in progress. Record venue, authorship, links, and the candidate's contribution exactly. Adapt summaries to the target prompt instead of pasting one paragraph everywhere.

## Recommenders

Choose recommenders who can give specific, relevant evidence and who satisfy the program's eligibility rules. Track:

- relationship and work observed
- themes they can support
- programs and deadlines
- request, reminder, and submission events
- packet provided: CV, transcript, statement draft, research summary, deadlines, and submission instructions as appropriate

Ask with enough lead time for the actual deadline and relationship; do not rely on a universal number of weeks. Never draft or submit a letter in a recommender's voice unless explicitly authorized and permitted by the institution.

## Final checks

- every prompt component is answered
- official limits and file rules are satisfied
- claims and publication statuses match source entries
- institution, program, faculty, and lab names are correct
- concurrently required statements are distinct
- recommendation requirements and deadlines are current
- final files are clearly named and rendered before upload
