# Job Application and Interview Workflows

Use this reference for saved jobs, company research, application tracking, cover-letter inputs, and target-specific interview packs.

## Capture a job

Store two distinct layers.

### Raw snapshot

- company and role
- location and employment type
- source URL or source file
- capture date and, when known, posting ID
- full JD text or a lossless saved copy

### Analysis

- must-have and preferred requirements
- responsibilities and success signals
- constraints and eligibility
- requirement-to-evidence matrix with source paths
- gaps, risks, and questions
- decision: apply / research / deprioritize, with rationale

Never edit the raw snapshot to make the candidate appear more aligned.

## Application tracking

Treat status as an event history, not only a mutable label. Recommended fields:

- company, role, source, and canonical job-record path
- current status
- last event date
- next action and due date
- tailored resume/cover-letter paths
- interview-pack path
- notes

Use explicit statuses such as `saved`, `preparing`, `submitted`, `assessment`, `interview-1`, `interview-2`, `offer`, `rejected`, `withdrawn`, or the workspace's existing vocabulary. Record status only from user-provided or directly observed evidence.

## Company research

Separate stable company research from role-specific JD analysis. For time-sensitive facts, record source and retrieval date. Prefer official company, team, product, engineering, filing, or program sources; label external commentary separately.

## Tailored materials

Build materials from the evidence matrix:

- **resume:** select and order proven signals
- **cover letter:** connect a small number of verified experiences to the role; avoid repeating the resume
- **short answers:** answer the portal prompt exactly and respect character limits
- **portfolio links:** include only accessible, relevant, and accurately described artifacts

Never claim company knowledge that was not researched.

## Interview pack

Create or update:

- `jd.md`: raw JD or link to its canonical job record
- `mock.md`: prioritized questions, answer outlines, evidence anchors, and follow-ups
- `my-q.md`: questions grouped by role outcomes, team execution, technical constraints, and growth
- optional domain notes when the JD genuinely requires them

Prioritize questions by likely relevance and evidence risk:

1. role motivation and fit
2. strongest source-backed experience
3. gaps or transitions the interviewer may probe
4. role-specific technical or case questions
5. collaboration, failure, and decision examples

Answers should sound spoken. Use a concise situation/context, the candidate's action, evidence-backed result, and reflection when helpful; do not force a rigid framework into every answer.

## Validation

- JD snapshot is preserved and dated.
- Every fit claim maps to a source entry.
- Company facts have sources and dates when time-sensitive.
- Application status is not inferred from prepared materials.
- Resume, cover letter, and interview answers agree on dates, titles, and ownership.
