# Interview Workspace

- `interview.md`: reusable introductions, experience stories, and technical topics
- `coding/`: coding drills and implementation debriefs
- `templates/`: JD-link, mock, and interviewer-question templates
- `<target>/`: company- or role-specific interview packs

Link target packs to the canonical JD under `jobs/saved/` to avoid drift. Prioritize questions by role relevance and evidence risk; answers should name their sources and claim boundaries. After interviews, record new facts in the relevant job or company file before promoting verified information into reusable material.
