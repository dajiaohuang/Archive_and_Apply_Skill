# Job Application Workspace

- `targets.md`: current role directions, constraints, and search strategy
- `saved/`: raw JD snapshots, evidence mapping, and application decisions
- `companies/`: sourced and dated company research
- `comparisons/`: cross-role comparisons
- `applications.md`: application events and current status
- `templates/`: job-record and company-research templates

Preserve the raw JD and capture metadata before analysis. Prepared materials do not prove submission; application status needs user confirmation or a visible record.
